# Umbrella Corporation – Project Management Simulation Context

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document serves as a reusable context pack for an ongoing educational Project Management simulation case study.

It is intended to be copied into ChatGPT at the start of any session to restore full project context without needing prior conversation history.

The goal of the simulation is to teach Project Management (PMBOK/PMI-based) concepts using a fictional but structured corporate scenario.

---

## 2. Project Overview

This is a fictional enterprise-level project initiated by Umbrella Corporation in an alternate timeline.

The project is framed as a strategic initiative to restore global leadership and corporate survival after a catastrophic global event.

### Core Narrative Background:
- In 1998, a T-Virus outbreak caused a global pandemic.
- Most of humanity was lost.
- Umbrella Corporation collapsed and lost its market dominance.
- By 2003, the corporation faced near extinction.
- In 2014, time travel technology was discovered by Dr. Vladimir Degtyarov’s team.
- Umbrella Corporation initiated a strategic initiative to change history by preventing the original outbreak.

---

## 3. Project Objective (Business Goal)

The primary business objective is:

> Restore Umbrella Corporation to its former global leadership position in pharmaceutical, bio-chemistry, and military contracting industries by altering past events (preventing the T-Virus outbreak).

This is treated as a strategic transformation initiative at enterprise level.

---

## 4. Project Constraints

- Only one attempt is allowed to execute the mission.
- Limited surviving corporate resources.
- Small, highly specialized team only.
- Limited training time (approx. 3 months ±10%).
- No possibility of mission rollback or repetition.
- Civilian casualties must be minimized.
- Operations must remain covert.
- External interference from autonomous systems (e.g., “Red Queen” AI system) is expected.

---

## 5. Key Assumptions

- Time travel technology is operational and reliable.
- Umbrella Corporation retains partial infrastructure and funding.
- Selected personnel can be trained within project timeline.
- Project has executive-level priority over all other initiatives.
- Cross-functional team training is feasible within constraints.
- Strategic success outweighs operational complexity.

---

## 6. Project Management Focus

This simulation must be treated as a real-world project management case study.

All outputs must follow PMBOK-style structure and thinking.

Expected artifacts include:

- Project Charter
- Business Case
- Stakeholder Register
- RACI Matrix
- Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- Risk Register (RAID Log)
- Communication Plan
- Change Management Plan
- Procurement Strategy
- Schedule (High-Level + Detailed)
- Budget Estimate
- Lessons Learned Register

---

## 7. Tone and Methodology

All outputs must be:

- Formal and corporate in tone
- Structured like enterprise PM documentation
- Based on project management best practices
- Focused on governance, scope, risk, and execution
- Free from narrative storytelling unless used as scenario context

---

## 8. Simulation Rules

- Fictional elements (zombies, AI systems, time travel) are treated as project constraints or environmental risks.
- The simulation must NOT devolve into tactical or combat analysis.
- All challenges must be translated into project management terminology.
- Every scenario must map to PM concepts (risk, scope, cost, schedule, resources, quality).

---

## 9. Expansion Capability

This context supports expansion into:

- Multi-phase project lifecycle simulation
- Crisis injection events (scope change, risk escalation, stakeholder conflict)
- Parallel project portfolios within Umbrella Corporation
- Advanced PM training scenarios for students
- Full PMO simulation environment

---

and continue generating project management artifacts without re-asking for background.
