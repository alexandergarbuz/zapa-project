# 2. Project Purpose and Justification

The collapse of global civilization following the 1998 T-Virus outbreak resulted in the severe degradation of Umbrella Corporation’s operational capacity, financial stability, and global influence.

Traditional recovery strategies have been deemed infeasible under current environmental conditions.

Therefore, the organization is pursuing a strategic intervention approach using experimentally validated time travel technology to alter the historical root cause of systemic failure.

---

# 3. Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Prevent the original release of the T-Virus
- Alter the historical timeline to avoid global collapse
- Restore Umbrella Corporation’s position as a global leader in:
  - Pharmaceuticals
  - Bio-chemistry
  - Advanced defense systems

---

# 4. High-Level Scope

## 4.1 In Scope

- Development of temporal intervention strategy
- Selection and training of tactical deployment team
- Operational planning for pre-outbreak insertion
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Preparation of mission execution framework

## 4.2 Out of Scope

- Post-intervention global reconstruction
- Long-term governance of restored timeline
- Large-scale military conflict operations
- Public disclosure or transparency initiatives

---

# 5. High-Level Requirements

- Establish a fully validated mission plan prior to execution
- Train selected tactical personnel for covert operations
- Ensure mission can be executed in a single attempt only
- Maintain strict operational secrecy at all times
- Ensure mission deployment occurs before outbreak event

---

# 6. Assumptions

- Time travel technology is operational and stable
- Required technical expertise is available within organization
- Selected personnel can be trained within defined timeline
- Historical target environment remains accessible and predictable
- No external temporal interference during planning phase

---

# 7. Constraints

- Single execution attempt (no rollback or retry possible)
- Limited operational timeframe (3 months ±10%)
- Restricted personnel pool
- High secrecy and containment requirements
- Mission must precede outbreak event

---

# 8. High-Level Risks

- Failure of temporal insertion system
- Detection or interference in target timeline
- Autonomous system interference (AI security system: “Red Queen”)
- Insufficient team readiness due to compressed training schedule
- Irreversible mission failure due to single-attempt constraint

---

# 9. Milestone Summary

- M1: Project authorization approved  
- M2: Core team selected  
- M3: Training and simulation completed  
- M4: Mission plan validated  
- M5: Deployment readiness confirmed  

---

# 10. Project Authority

---

Approval of this charter authorizes formal initiation of the Z.A.P.A. project and allocation of organizational resources for planning and preparation activities.
