# 1. Risk Scoring Methodology

## 1.1 Scale Definition

### Probability (P)
- 1 = Rare (<10%)
- 2 = Unlikely (10–30%)
- 3 = Possible (30–50%)
- 4 = Likely (50–70%)
- 5 = Almost Certain (>70%)

### Impact (I)
- 1 = Negligible
- 2 = Minor
- 3 = Moderate
- 4 = Major
- 5 = Critical (project failure level)

### Risk Score = P × I

---

# 2. Risk Matrix Categories

- 1–5   → Low Risk
- 6–10  → Medium Risk
- 11–15 → High Risk
- 16–25 → Critical Risk

---

# 5. Risk Response Strategy Summary

- Avoid: Structural failure risks where possible
- Mitigate: Technical and operational risks via redundancy and training
- Transfer: Limited applicability (external environment cannot be transferred)
- Accept: Unknown environmental variables in timeline

---

# 6. Monitoring & Control

- Weekly risk review by PMO
- Real-time monitoring for system-level risks
- Executive escalation required for any Critical-level risk (>16)
- Risk register updated continuously throughout planning phase
