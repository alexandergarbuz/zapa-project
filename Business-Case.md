# Zombie Apocalypse Preparedness Analysis (Z.A.P.A.)

## 1.0 Introduction / Background

In 1998, the outbreak of the T-Virus caused a global pandemic and resulted in most of the human population being turned into zombies. As a result, most humans died, and Umbrella Corporation lost most of its assets by 2003.

The corporation lost its position as a global leader and, more importantly, faced near extinction due to the collapse of human society and the loss of all revenue sources.

---

## 2.0 Business Objective

The main business objective of this project is to return Umbrella Corporation to its former position as a globally recognized leader in pharmaceutical, bio-chemistry, and military contracting industries.

However, this cannot be achieved within the current environment. The organization must change the environment itself by altering history and preventing the original T-Virus outbreak. This would allow restoration of its prior strategic position.

---

## 3.0 Current Situation and Problem / Opportunity Statement

Since the discovery of time travel technology in September 2014 by Dr. Vladimir Degtyarov’s team, the executive leadership has focused on consolidating remaining corporate assets to support this initiative.

According to the CEO’s weekly communication issued in November 2014, this project represents a critical opportunity to restore Umbrella Corporation’s position and potentially exceed its former global influence if successfully executed.

---

## 4.0 Critical Assumptions and Constraints

### 4.1 Key Assumptions

The following assumptions have been made in order to proceed with the project:

1. This project has priority over all other initiatives.
2. The company retains sufficient assets to support execution.
3. The majority of remaining resources can be allocated to this initiative.
4. Civilian casualties can be minimized or avoided.
5. The central AI system (“Red Queen”) can be disabled or contained.

---

### 4.2 Core Constraints

The following constraints will impact project execution:

1. The operation must be completed before the T-Virus outbreak.
2. Only one execution attempt is permitted.
3. Available personnel are limited, requiring a small specialized team.
4. Significant cross-training is required due to varying experience levels.
5. Mission design must avoid large-scale military operations due to resource limitations.

---

## 5.0 Analysis of Options and Recommendation

### 5.1 Options

Two primary options are under consideration:

- **Option 1:** Proceed with the project, allocating significant resources while suspending or canceling non-critical initiatives.
- **Option 2:** Cancel the project and allocate remaining resources toward corporate security and stabilization.

---

### 5.2 Analysis

Option 2 presents a lower immediate risk profile but does not support long-term strategic objectives. Additionally, continued degradation of resources suggests that delaying action may eliminate future feasibility of the initiative.

Option 1 presents higher execution risk but is the only viable path toward achieving strategic recovery.

---

### 5.3 Recommendation

It is recommended to proceed with **Option 1**, supported by comprehensive planning, risk mitigation strategies, and strict execution governance.

---

## 6.0 Preliminary Project Requirements

The following high-level requirements have been identified:

1. The project team must develop a comprehensive project plan including schedule, resource inventory, and risk management strategy.
2. Tactical and support teams must receive adequate training to operate covertly and independently.
3. Civilian casualties must be minimized where possible.
4. Mission execution must occur before the T-Virus outbreak, requiring strict adherence to planning timelines.
5. Once execution begins, the mission cannot be aborted or reattempted.

---

## 7.0 Budget Estimate and Financial Analysis

The project budget is not yet fully defined; however, it is expected to be substantial.

The majority of costs will be associated with:
- Specialized equipment procurement
- Training and preparation of personnel
- Time travel system operational requirements
- Mission-critical logistics and support infrastructure

A detailed cost breakdown will be provided following completion of team structure analysis and equipment requirements definition.

---

## 8.0 Schedule Estimate

The project schedule is constrained by both operational urgency and environmental instability.

While planning activities must be thorough and comprehensive, the preparation phase must remain limited in duration due to ongoing security threats and operational pressures.

It is estimated that the total planning, training, and preparation phase should not exceed:

**3 months ± 10%**

---

## 9.0 Potential Risks

The following key risks have been identified:

1. Critical mission elements may be overlooked during the planning phase, impacting execution success.
2. Equipment or system failures during execution may compromise mission objectives.
3. The “Red Queen” AI system may actively interfere with mission operations during infiltration and execution phases.

Further risk analysis will be conducted as part of the formal Risk Management Plan, and appropriate mitigation strategies will be developed.
