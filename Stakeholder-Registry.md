# 4. Key Engagement Principles

- Executive stakeholders require high-frequency reporting and decision validation checkpoints.
- Technical stakeholders require collaborative integration with minimal administrative overhead.
- Operational teams require structured communication and mission clarity.
- Autonomous systems must be treated as dynamic risk actors, not traditional stakeholders.
